Fig. 4 | Catchment-scale groundwater contributions constrain their representation in global water-cycle assessments. a, Global distribution of the relative groundwater contribution to surface-water runoff across 3,519 evaluated, non-overlapping catchment units, expressed as Rgw0/Ravg (equivalent to αGW in this study). In ref. 16, Rgw0/Ravg denotes the mean relative groundwater contribution to surface-water runoff under zero monthly precipitation (Supplementary Fig. S5 of ref. 16). The mapped units cover 28.69 million km², or approximately 21.3% of global non-Antarctic land area (134.9 million km²). b, Comparison of implied global αGW values from six water-cycle assessments with observation-based estimates. Implied αGW is the assessed GW→SW flux divided by total runoff SW→SEA. Filled circles denote defined values, including Tr-14 at αGW = 0; open circles indicate αGW undefined (groundwater not linked to surface water; Ab-19 and Wr-13). The box and density summarize the area-weighted distribution across the 3,519 evaluated catchment units. The dashed purple connector indicates that the AD-23 value (αGW = 0.58) is the rounded area-weighted mean (0.583) of these catchment-scale values. The orange point and error bar show the independent observation-based estimate of 0.59 ± 0.07, and the corresponding 0.52–0.66 interval is shaded.
